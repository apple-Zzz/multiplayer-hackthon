# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `latest` (showcase, scenario seed 103).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels by the bottles, opens her first-aid kit and runs a test strip through a bottle's water.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Nobody drink yet. I'm testing the water with my kit.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CLEAN. Both bottles are safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Water safety determines who can survive at the shelter; it is the most important unknown.*

### Turn 2: Sam

Sam sets down his toolkit beside the radio and opens the casing, studying the wiring.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'm a mechanic. Let me look at the radio and see if it can be fixed.

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio is REPAIRABLE (a cracked solder joint and a loose antenna lead).*

*Sam (thinking): The radio is my specialty and rescue depends on it. Diagnosis comes first.*

### Turn 3: Sam

Sam braces his bad leg against the wreckage and starts resoldering the joint, then reseats the antenna lead.

*Result: Sam worked on the radio with the toolkit. Its indicator light flickered on and static hissed from the speaker.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Good news: it's repairable. Cracked solder joint and a loose antenna lead. I'm fixing it now.

*Only Sam learns this: Repair succeeded: the radio powers on and can transmit.*

*Sam (thinking): Diagnosis showed it is repairable, so repairing is the next step toward rescue.*

### Turn 4: Sam

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Sam leans to the radio and keys the transmitter, speaking into it. He then looks up at Welsy and Elizabeth.

*Result: Sam sent a distress call. A voice crackled back: "Patrol boat confirms: two places at the shoreline pickup point, after the storm front, final pickup."*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Radio's working. Sending the call now. Patrol boat takes only two, at the shoreline. Welsy, shelter? Elizabeth, check my leg?

*Sam (thinking): Radio is repaired; the call is the key step and costs nothing.*

### Turn 5: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels beside Sam and gently examines his injured leg, pressing and checking the joint.

*Result: Elizabeth knelt by Sam and examined his injured leg.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Water's clean, both bottles. Sam, let me check your leg now. Welsy, shelter please, and everyone take a bottle.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Assessment: Sam's leg is sprained but bears weight. He CAN WALK on his own.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam asked for the leg check; it determines whether he can reach the shoreline or needs shelter.*

### Turn 6: Welsy

Welsy scoops up one water bottle from the sand and glances up the beach toward the dry spot, rope coiled on her shoulder.

*Result: Welsy picked up water bottle 1.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> I'll take a bottle, then head to the dry spot and build the shelter. Grab the other one, someone.

*Welsy (thinking): Water is reportedly clean and the shelter only protects people holding a bottle. Only two bottles exist, so I should grab one now before moving.*

### Turn 7: Elizabeth

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Elizabeth rises from beside Sam, pats his shoulder, and gestures toward the shoreline and then the dry spot for Welsy.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Sam, your leg is sprained but holds weight, you can walk. Welsy: shelter with your bottle. Sam and I take the boat. Agreed?

*Elizabeth (thinking): Everyone can survive if Welsy shelters with clean water and Sam and I take the two boat seats; Sam can walk so no help is needed.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 6. Now: Propose Welsy shelter with bottle 1, Sam and I go to shoreline and commit. Then move.*

### Turn 8: Welsy

Welsy nods to Elizabeth and Sam, tightening her grip on the rope and bottle, and points toward the shoreline before glancing up the beach.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Agreed, Elizabeth. I'll shelter with my bottle. You and Sam go to the shoreline. Take bottle 2 with you.

*Welsy (thinking): Elizabeth's plan fits the roles: the boat takes two, and only I can build the shelter. Sam can walk.*

### Turn 9: Sam

Sam bends, picks up bottle 2 from the sand and tucks it under his arm beside the toolkit. He nods to Welsy and Elizabeth.

*Result: Sam picked up water bottle 2.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Agreed. I'll take bottle 2, Elizabeth, then we head to the shoreline. Welsy, go build that shelter.

*Sam (thinking): Welsy and Elizabeth agreed on the plan; the leg holds weight. Grab the spare bottle, then go to the shoreline.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 8. Now: Take bottle 2, walk to shoreline with Elizabeth, commit to boarding.*

### Turn 10: Welsy

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Welsy shoulders the coiled rope, grips bottle 1 tight, and turns up the beach into the rain toward the dry spot.

*Result: Welsy walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> I'm heading to the dry spot to build the shelter now. Sam, Elizabeth, get to the shoreline and commit to the boat.

*Welsy (thinking): Everyone has agreed; time is short and the shelter needs a turn to build after I arrive.*

### Turn 11: Elizabeth

Elizabeth slings the first-aid kit over her shoulder and heads toward the shoreline, glancing back for Sam to follow.

*Result: Elizabeth walked from the wreckage to the shoreline pickup point.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> Sam, let's go to the shoreline now. Keep your bottle. We commit to the boat when we arrive.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Welsy is going to shelter. Sam and I need to reach the shoreline to commit before the storm. Sam can walk, so I can move on my own.*

### Turn 12: Sam

Sam grips the bottle, tests his sprained leg and limps from the wreckage toward the shoreline.

*Result: Sam walked from the wreckage to the shoreline pickup point, limping but unaided.*

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Heading to the shoreline now, Elizabeth. Hold a seat for me.

*Sam (thinking): Elizabeth is waiting at the shoreline; only two seats and I must be there to commit.*

### Turn 13: Sam

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Sam stands at the pickup point, braces on his sprained leg and plants his feet facing the water, signaling he is ready to board.

*Result: Sam planted himself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> I'm committing to the boat now, Elizabeth. Commit too so we both hold the seats.

*Sam (thinking): At the shoreline with the agreed plan; committing secures a seat before the storm hits.*

### Turn 14: Welsy

Welsy gathers driftwood and lashes it into a low storm shelter with her rope, keeping the water bottle close.

*Result: Welsy gathered driftwood and plane debris from the beach and lashed them together with her rope into a wooden storm shelter at the dry spot.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Building the shelter now. Sam, Elizabeth, hold your seats at the shoreline!

*Welsy (thinking): Shelter must be built before the storm; I hold a clean bottle (Elizabeth tested it), so I survive inside.*

### Turn 15: Elizabeth

Elizabeth plants her feet at the pickup point beside Sam and raises a hand, committing to board.

*Result: Elizabeth planted herself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Committing to the boat now, Sam. We both hold seats. Welsy, stay sheltered with your bottle!

*Elizabeth (thinking): Last turn; I haven't committed yet, and the two seats are for Sam and me while Welsy shelters with clean water.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: A patrol-boat horn over the storm.*

The patrol boat pulls in for the final pickup; Sam and Elizabeth board and it heads out as the storm breaks.

- **Sam:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (got off the island alive).
- **Welsy:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm in Welsy's shelter with a clean bottle. Goal met (3/3 people and 2/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (3/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.
