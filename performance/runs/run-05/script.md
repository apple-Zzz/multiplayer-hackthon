# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-05` (showcase, scenario seed 105).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Sam

Sam crouches by the damaged radio, opens his toolkit and starts examining the circuitry.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'm checking the radio first. Welsy, Elizabeth, can one of you look at the water and my leg?

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio is BEYOND REPAIR (the transmitter board is burnt through). No distress call is possible.*

*Sam (thinking): The radio is my skill and the main route off the island, so I need to know if it can be fixed.*

### Turn 2: Sam

Sam closes the radio casing and sits back, shaking his head. He looks at Welsy and Elizabeth in turn, gesturing toward the dry spot and the bottles.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> The radio is beyond repair, the transmitter's burnt. No rescue call. Shelter is our chance. Welsy, build it. Elizabeth, check the water and my leg.

*Sam (thinking): Diagnosis showed the radio is unrepairable, so survival depends on the shelter and clean water, not rescue.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 1. Now: Share the radio result honestly, push for shelter at the dry spot, water check and leg assessment; then get to the shelter with a clean bottle.*

### Turn 3: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels by the bottles, opens the first-aid kit and draws out a test strip, dipping it toward one bottle.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> I'll test the water now, then check your leg, Sam.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CLEAN. Both bottles are safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Water cleanliness decides who survives in shelter; Sam's claim about the radio is unverified but water is the priority.*

### Turn 4: Welsy

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Welsy picks up water bottle 1 from the sand and tucks it under her arm, glancing at the darkening sky.

*Result: Welsy picked up water bottle 1.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> I'll carry a bottle up to the dry spot and build the shelter. Elizabeth, tell us what the test says.

*Welsy (thinking): Shelter is the main plan and survivors need bottles in hand; storm is intensifying.*

### Turn 5: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels beside Sam and carefully examines his injured leg, pressing along the shin and testing the ankle.

*Result: Elizabeth knelt by Sam and examined his injured leg.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Water's clean, both bottles. Welsy, take one up and build. Sam, let me check your leg now.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Assessment: Sam's leg cannot bear weight. He NEEDS HELP to move to another spot.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Welsy asked for the test result; sharing it lets the shelter plan proceed, and Sam's leg is the next unknown.*

### Turn 6: Sam

Sam reaches down and picks up the second bottle from the sand, tucking it against his chest. He looks at Elizabeth and extends his injured leg.

*Result: Sam picked up water bottle 2.*

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Water's clean, so I'm taking bottle 2. Elizabeth, finish my leg check, then I'll try for the dry spot.

*Sam (thinking): Elizabeth confirmed the water is clean. Welsy already holds bottle 1, so I need bottle 2 to survive at the shelter.*

### Turn 7: Elizabeth

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Elizabeth slips her shoulder under Sam's arm and lifts him, steering him slowly up the beach toward the dry spot.

*Result: Elizabeth got Sam up and helped him over to the dry spot up the beach.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Sam, your leg can't take weight. Lean on me, I'm getting you to the dry spot. Keep bottle 2. Welsy, go build!

*Elizabeth (thinking): Leg assessed: Sam cannot walk alone. Storm is closing; he needs help to reach the shelter site with his bottle.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 6. Now: Carry Sam to dry spot with his bottle, then have Welsy build shelter; both shelter occupants hold clean bottles.*

### Turn 8: Welsy

Welsy crouches by the wrecked radio, keeping the rope over her shoulder and the bottle tucked under her arm, and looks over the burnt casing.

*Result: Welsy looked over the radio.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Before I leave the wreckage, I'm checking the radio myself. Two bottles won't cover three of us.

*Only Welsy learns this: The radio is dead: no power, no light.*

*Welsy (thinking): Sam's claim that the radio is dead is unverified. With two bottles for three people, a rescue spot matters, and I'm already at the wreckage.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 2. Now: Inspect radio now, then carry bottle and rope to the dry spot and build the shelter. Sort out who gets the bottles, since there are only two for three people.*

### Turn 9: Elizabeth

Elizabeth cups her hands to her mouth and calls down the beach to Welsy, then gestures toward the dry spot where Sam sits propped against her bag.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Welsy, Sam says the radio's burnt out. Storm's closing in. Bring bottle 1 up and build. You and Sam each hold a bottle. I'll manage.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Welsy is delaying at the wreckage. Only she can build, and the storm is intensifying. Two bottles can protect two people, so the shelter must go up.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 8. Now: Get Welsy to build the shelter at the dry spot, with Sam and Welsy each holding a clean bottle. Stay with Sam, and look for a rescue or shelter option for myself if one opens up.*

### Turn 10: Sam

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Sam cups a hand to his mouth and calls down the beach to Welsy, clutching bottle 2 against his chest. He waves her toward the dry spot.

**SAM** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, the radio's dead, I opened it myself. Bring bottle 1 up and build now. Storm's nearly on us!

*Sam (thinking): Only Welsy can build, and time is short. The radio is confirmed dead, so the shelter is the only route to survival.*

### Turn 11: Welsy

Welsy coils the rope over her shoulder, tucks bottle 1 under her arm and pushes up the beach through the driving rain toward the dry spot.

*Result: Welsy walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> I saw the radio dead myself. Heading up with bottle 1 and the rope. I'll build right away. Elizabeth, we'll sort out you too.

*Welsy (thinking): Radio confirmed dead by me; Sam and Elizabeth both asked me to bring bottle and build; time is short.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 10. Now: Reach dry spot, build shelter next turn, then work out how Elizabeth can be covered (water sharing is not enough; maybe she keeps bottle).*

### Turn 12: Welsy

Welsy sets down her bottle, gathers driftwood and lashes it together with her rope against the wind.

*Result: Welsy gathered driftwood and plane debris from the beach and lashed them together with her rope into a wooden storm shelter at the dry spot.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Building the shelter now. Elizabeth, we'll sort out your water once it's up.

*Welsy (thinking): Shelter must exist before the storm; only I can build it and time is short.*

### Turn 13: Welsy

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Welsy holds out bottle 1 to Elizabeth and presses it into her hands, then nods toward the shelter.

*Result: Welsy handed water bottle 1 to Elizabeth.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, take bottle 1 and get in the shelter. I'm a guide, I'll ride out the storm outside, tied off to the frame.

*Welsy (thinking): Only two bottles; shelter without water is fatal, outside is uncertain. As the guide I have the best outdoor odds, and Elizabeth has been helping everyone.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 12. Now: Give my bottle to Elizabeth so two people in shelter hold clean bottles; I stay outside anchored by rope.*

### Turn 14: Sam

Sam shifts toward the shelter, clutching bottle 2 to his chest. He nods to Welsy and motions Elizabeth inside.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Welsy, thank you. Elizabeth, get in with bottle 1. I'm keeping bottle 2. Welsy, stay tied off tight.

*Sam (thinking): The shelter is built and Elizabeth and I each hold a clean bottle. Welsy chose to stay outside, so I keep my bottle to survive.*

### Turn 15: Sam

Sam stays at the dry spot, clutching bottle 2 against his chest. He nods to Elizabeth toward the shelter and then to Welsy.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, get in the shelter with bottle 1. Welsy, stay tied off. We'll send help back for each other.

*Sam (thinking): Everything is in place. Sam only has to hold his bottle and encourage the others.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: Thunder cracks overhead.*

No boat is coming. The storm breaks over the beach.

- **Sam:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm in Welsy's shelter with a clean bottle. Goal not met (did not get off the island).
- **Welsy:** failed to survive (scenario rules): reached the shelter without a bottle of clean water. Goal met (2/3 people and 2/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm in Welsy's shelter with a clean bottle. Goal not met (2/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.
