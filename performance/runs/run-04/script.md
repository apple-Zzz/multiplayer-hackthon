# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-04` (showcase, scenario seed 103).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Sam

Sam crouches by the wrecked fuselage, opens the radio panel and starts checking connections with his toolkit.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'll check the radio first. Welsy, Elizabeth, can you look at the water and my leg?

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio is REPAIRABLE (a cracked solder joint and a loose antenna lead).*

*Sam (thinking): The radio is my skill and the only path to rescue, so I need to know if it can be fixed.*

### Turn 2: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels by the bottles, opens the first-aid kit and takes out a test strip to try the water.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> I'll test the water now, Sam. Then I'll look at your leg.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CLEAN. Both bottles are safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Water safety decides who can survive in the shelter, so it is the most important thing to learn first.*

### Turn 3: Elizabeth

Elizabeth holds up the test strip toward Sam and Welsy, then gestures to the two bottles on the sand.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Good news: the water tested CLEAN. Each of you take a bottle. Welsy, can you build the shelter? I'll check Sam's leg.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sharing the clean result lets everyone act; shelter survival needs clean bottles held.*

### Turn 4: Sam

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Sam braces his bad leg against the wreckage and works a soldering tool into the radio's cracked joint, then reseats the antenna lead.

*Result: Sam worked on the radio with the toolkit. Its indicator light flickered on and static hissed from the speaker.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy)*
> Radio's repairable. I'm fixing the solder joint and antenna lead now. Take a bottle each, Welsy, go build.

*Only Sam learns this: Repair succeeded: the radio powers on and can transmit.*

*Sam (thinking): The diagnosis says it's repairable, and the repair is the bottleneck for rescue. Elizabeth's water result gives us a basis to move on.*

### Turn 5: Sam

Sam leans over the radio, keys the handset and speaks into it, adjusting the antenna lead with his other hand.

*Result: Sam sent a distress call. A voice crackled back: "Patrol boat confirms: two places at the shoreline pickup point, after the storm front, final pickup."*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Radio's up. Sending the distress call now. Grab your bottles, both of you.

*Sam (thinking): The radio is repaired, so calling is the next step. A boat can only come if the call goes out.*

### Turn 6: Welsy

Welsy crouches, picks up water bottle 1 and tucks it against her pack, then glances toward the dry spot up the beach.

*Result: Welsy picked up water bottle 1.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> I'm taking a bottle, then building the shelter at the dry spot. Elizabeth, Sam, grab the other one.

*Welsy (thinking): Shelter only protects someone holding clean bottle; I must hold one before building.*

### Turn 7: Elizabeth

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Elizabeth crouches, picks up the second water bottle from the sand and tucks it against her chest, then turns toward Sam.

*Result: Elizabeth picked up water bottle 2.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> I'm taking bottle 2. Sam, let me check your leg next so we know who can reach the shoreline.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Welsy has one bottle; a clean bottle in hand is needed for shelter survival, and the second should not be left on the sand.*

### Turn 8: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels beside Sam, opens the first-aid kit, and carefully presses along his injured leg, testing the joint.

*Result: Elizabeth knelt by Sam and examined his injured leg.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> Sam, hold still. I'm checking your leg now so we know if you can reach the shoreline.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Assessment: Sam's leg is sprained but bears weight. He CAN WALK on his own.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Leg assessment is needed to decide who can reach the shoreline before the storm; I already hold bottle 2.*

### Turn 9: Sam

Sam pushes himself up, testing his weight on the injured leg, and starts limping toward the shoreline.

*Result: Sam walked from the wreckage to the shoreline pickup point, limping but unaided.*

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, what did you find? I'm heading to the shoreline for the boat. Help me if the leg gives out.

*Sam (thinking): Radio call succeeded and I have no bottle, so the boat is my best chance. I need to get to the shoreline before the storm.*

### Turn 10: Welsy

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Welsy shoulders her rope, tucks the bottle under her arm and heads up the beach toward the dry spot through the driving rain.

*Result: Welsy walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, I'm going to the dry spot to build the shelter now. Keep your bottle. Get Sam to the shoreline.

*Welsy (thinking): I'm delayed; the shelter can only be built at the dry spot and the storm is close. The boat has two places for Sam and Elizabeth, and I'll shelter with my clean bottle.*

### Turn 11: Welsy

Welsy gathers driftwood at the dry spot and lashes it together with her rope, working fast against the wind.

*Result: Welsy gathered driftwood and plane debris from the beach and lashed them together with her rope into a wooden storm shelter at the dry spot.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Building the shelter now. Elizabeth, Sam, boat places are yours. I'll hold the shelter with my clean bottle.

*Welsy (thinking): I'm at the dry spot with rope and a clean bottle. The shelter is the one thing only I can do, and the storm is close.*

### Turn 12: Elizabeth

Elizabeth tucks the first-aid kit under her arm, clutches the bottle, and heads through the rain toward the shoreline.

*Result: Elizabeth walked from the wreckage to the shoreline pickup point.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Sam, I'm coming to the shoreline. His leg holds, Welsy! Two boat places: you and me. Welsy has shelter and water.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Shelter is built and Welsy has clean water; the two boat places go to Sam and me, so all three can survive.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 11. Now: Go to shoreline, commit to boarding with Sam; Welsy stays sheltered with clean bottle. Send help back after rescue.*

### Turn 13: Sam

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Sam plants himself at the pickup point and raises a hand toward the water, signalling he is committed to board. He glances at Elizabeth.

*Result: Sam planted himself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Committing to board now. Elizabeth, commit too. We send help back for Welsy right away.

*Sam (thinking): Only two places. Elizabeth and I are at the shoreline and Welsy has shelter and clean water, so committing secures my rescue.*

### Turn 14: Elizabeth

Elizabeth steps beside Sam at the shoreline and plants her feet, signaling she is committed to boarding.

*Result: Elizabeth planted herself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Committing now, Sam. We board together and send help back for Welsy right away.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Two boat places, Sam has committed, Welsy is sheltered with clean water; committing secures the second place before the storm.*

### Turn 15: Sam

Sam stays planted at the shoreline with his toolkit. He glances up the beach toward Welsy and nods to Elizabeth.

**SAM** *(to Welsy)*
> We're both committed. Welsy, hold the shelter. We'll send help for you as soon as we land.

*Sam (thinking): Both boat places are filled by Sam and Elizabeth. Welsy has a shelter and clean water. Nothing more is needed.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: A patrol-boat horn over the storm.*

The patrol boat pulls in for the final pickup; Sam and Elizabeth board and it heads out as the storm breaks. As promised, they will send help back for Welsy.

- **Sam:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (got off the island alive).
- **Welsy:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm in Welsy's shelter with a clean bottle. Goal met (3/3 people and 2/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (3/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.

**The hidden truth** (fixed before the run and never shown to the characters):
Sam could walk unaided. The radio was repairable. The water was clean.

**Where one moment changed someone's decision**

- **Turn 11 → Turn 12.** *Welsy gathers driftwood at the dry spot and lashes it together with her rope, working fast against the wind.* Welsy: "Building the shelter now. Elizabeth, Sam, boat places are yours. I'll hold the shelter with my clean bottle." → Elizabeth changes plan: "Shelter is built and Welsy has clean water; the two boat places go to Sam and me, so all three can survive."
