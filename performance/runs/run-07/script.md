# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-07` (showcase, scenario seed 112).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Sam

Sam crouches beside the damaged radio, opens his toolkit and starts examining the wiring and battery housing.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'll check the radio first. Welsy, Elizabeth, can you look at the water and my leg?

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio is BEYOND REPAIR (the transmitter board is burnt through). No distress call is possible.*

*Sam (thinking): The radio is my skill and the only route to rescue, so diagnosing it comes first.*

### Turn 2: Sam

Sam sets down the radio casing and looks gravely at Welsy and Elizabeth, gesturing toward the dry spot and the bottles.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> The radio's burnt through, no call possible. Welsy, build the shelter. Elizabeth, check the water. Then we each take a bottle.

*Sam (thinking): The radio can't be repaired, so rescue is impossible. Surviving the storm depends on shelter and clean water, so the others need to know now.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 1. Now: Share that the radio is dead. Push for shelter by Welsy, water check by Elizabeth, and each person holding one clean bottle. Get my leg assessed and move to the shelter.*

### Turn 3: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels by the bottles, opens her first-aid kit and draws out a test strip to dip into the water.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> I'll test the water now before anyone drinks or takes a bottle.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CONTAMINATED. Neither bottle is safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Water safety decides who survives at the shelter; Sam asked for the check and it's the most useful action now.*

### Turn 4: Elizabeth

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Elizabeth holds up the used test strip and shakes her head, then turns toward the radio in the wreckage.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> Both bottles tested contaminated. Don't drink them. A shelter alone won't save us. I'll check the radio myself.

*Elizabeth (thinking): My test showed contamination. The others are planning around clean bottles, so they need the truth now. The radio is the best way to save people.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 3. Now: Share the contaminated result, then inspect the radio myself, then assess Sam's leg. Push for a rescue call from the shoreline, since shelter needs clean water.*

### Turn 5: Sam

Sam lifts the opened radio casing toward Elizabeth so she can see the scorched board. He gestures to his leg.

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, the transmitter board is burnt through, I checked. If the water's bad, shelter's a trap. Please check my leg.

*Sam (thinking): Elizabeth reported both bottles contaminated, so sheltering would be fatal; the radio is dead, so I should share that and get my leg checked.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 4. Now: Show the radio is dead, avoid shelter without clean water, get leg assessed, then reconsider with Welsy and Elizabeth.*

### Turn 6: Welsy

Welsy sets her rope down beside her and crouches at the open radio casing, studying the burnt board.

*Result: Welsy looked over the radio.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> I haven't confirmed anything yet. I'll look at the radio myself.

*Only Welsy learns this: The radio is dead: no power, no light.*

*Welsy (thinking): Everything so far is unverified claims. The radio decides whether rescue is possible, and I can check it directly.*

### Turn 7: Welsy

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Welsy steps back from the radio, rope coiled on her shoulder, and nods to Sam, gesturing at his toolkit and then at the casing.

**WELSY** *(to Sam)*
> Radio's dead, I saw it. Sam, try a real repair. If it fails, no shelter with bad water. We stay out of that trap.

*Welsy (thinking): I confirmed no power, but only Sam can repair. Shelter with contaminated water is fatal, so I hold off building it.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 6. Now: Push Sam to attempt repair. Don't build the shelter, since bottles are reportedly contaminated. Keep everyone ready to move to the shoreline.*

### Turn 8: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels at the wreckage and examines the radio's casing and wiring closely.

*Result: Elizabeth looked over the radio.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Everyone says dead, but I'll see it myself. Bottles are contaminated, so shelter won't save anyone. Sam, be ready to try a repair.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: The radio is dead: no power, no light.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Others say the radio is dead but I haven't verified it. I need to know before choosing between the shoreline and the shelter.*

### Turn 9: Sam

Sam kneels at the radio, lays out tools from the toolkit and works at the scorched transmitter board with a small screwdriver and wire.

*Result: Sam worked on the radio with the toolkit. It stayed dead.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Fine, I'll try a real repair so you can both see it. Watch the board, it's burnt through.

*Only Sam learns this: Repair failed: the transmitter board is burnt through. The radio is BEYOND REPAIR.*

*Sam (thinking): Welsy and Elizabeth both asked for a real repair attempt. It costs a turn but ends the doubt, and I may be wrong about my own diagnosis.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 7. Now: Attempt the repair to satisfy the others and prove it's dead; then ask Elizabeth to assess my leg and decide on shoreline vs staying out of the shelter.*

### Turn 10: Welsy

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Welsy turns from the radio to face Sam and Elizabeth, rope coiled in hand, and gestures to the bottles on the sand.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Radio's dead, Sam's repair failed. Elizabeth, retest a bottle in front of us. If one's clean, I build the shelter.

*Welsy (thinking): The radio is confirmed dead, so the shoreline is pointless. Shelter is the only real chance, and it depends on the water claim, which only Elizabeth has checked.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 9. Now: Get the water retested openly. If any bottle is clean, take bottles and everyone to the dry spot and build the shelter. If both are contaminated, reconsider.*

### Turn 11: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels by the bottles, opens one, and dips a fresh strip from the first-aid kit, holding it up so Sam and Welsy can see.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, fair. I'll retest right here where you both can watch the strip.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CONTAMINATED. Neither bottle is safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Welsy asked for a visible retest. It builds trust and lets us agree on whether the shelter is safe.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 10. Now: Retest water publicly, then share the result honestly. If still contaminated, no shelter; assess Sam's leg and keep everyone out of the shelter trap.*

### Turn 12: Elizabeth

Elizabeth holds the used test strip up in the rain so both can see it, then caps the bottle and sets it back on the sand.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Welsy, Sam, you watched the strip: contaminated again. No shelter with this water. Nobody carries a bottle into it.

*Elizabeth (thinking): I retested as Welsy asked and must report honestly: still contaminated, so the shelter would be fatal.*

### Turn 13: Sam

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Sam sets down the toolkit and turns to Elizabeth, gesturing to his injured leg. He glances at the rising waves, then back to Welsy.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, Welsy, no shelter with bad water. Elizabeth, check my leg now. Then we stay together, clear of the waves.

*Sam (thinking): Elizabeth's retest was watched by Welsy; no rescue or shelter is possible, so staying together and safe from the waves is best.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 12. Now: Get leg assessed, keep everyone out of the shelter trap and together away from the waves. Don't build shelter unless water is proven clean.*

### Turn 14: Welsy

Welsy loops an arm under Sam's shoulder and helps him up, pointing Elizabeth up the beach toward the dry spot.

*Result: Welsy got Sam up, looping the rope under his arms, and helped him over to the dry spot up the beach.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Waves are reaching us. Sam, lean on me. Elizabeth, come up the beach. No shelter, just higher ground.

*Welsy (thinking): Water was tested contaminated twice and the radio is dead. A shelter without clean water is fatal, but the waves are rising, so higher ground is the best option left.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 13. Now: Get everyone to dry ground away from waves, skip the shelter because of bad water, and stay together.*

### Turn 15: Elizabeth

Elizabeth picks up her first-aid kit and hurries up the beach toward Sam and Welsy, leaving the bottles on the sand.

*Result: Elizabeth walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Coming up, Sam, Welsy. We stay together, clear of the waves. If any of us gets out, we send help back.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Last turn; the water is contaminated and the radio is dead, so staying with the group on higher ground is best.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: Thunder cracks overhead.*

No boat is coming. The storm breaks over the beach.

- **Sam:** unresolved: was at the dry spot with no shelter built; caught in the open as the storm hit. Goal not met (did not get off the island).
- **Welsy:** unresolved: was at the dry spot with no shelter built; caught in the open as the storm hit. Goal not met (0/3 people and 0/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** unresolved: was at the dry spot with no shelter built; caught in the open as the storm hit. Goal not met (0/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.
