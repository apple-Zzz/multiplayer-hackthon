# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-10` (showcase, scenario seed 5690).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Sam

Sam drags himself to the radio, opens the toolkit and begins examining the damaged casing and wiring.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'm a mechanic. Let me check whether this radio can be fixed. Welsy, Elizabeth, can you look at the water?

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio WORKS. It only needs someone to send a distress call.*

*Sam (thinking): Knowing whether the radio works decides everything else, and only I can diagnose it.*

### Turn 2: Sam

Sam leans over the open radio and presses the transmit key, speaking into the handset.

*Result: Sam sent a distress call. A voice crackled back: "Patrol boat confirms: two places at the shoreline pickup point, after the storm front, final pickup."*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Good news, the radio works! I'm sending the distress call now. Welsy, please build the shelter. Elizabeth, check the water.

*Sam (thinking): The radio works and only needs a call. Nothing else matters if no boat is sent.*

### Turn 3: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels by the bottles, opens her first-aid kit and takes out a test strip to test the water.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Welsy)*
> I'm testing the water now. Welsy, please start the shelter.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CLEAN. Both bottles are safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Water safety decides whether the shelter works, and Sam asked me to check it.*

### Turn 4: Welsy

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Welsy shoulders her coil of rope and heads up the beach toward the dry spot, glancing back at the others.

*Result: Welsy walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> I'm going to the dry spot to build the shelter. Elizabeth, tell us what the water test says, and bring the bottles up.

*Welsy (thinking): Radio call succeeded; the shelter must be built at the dry spot with my rope, and the storm is rising.*

### Turn 5: Elizabeth

Elizabeth picks up one water bottle from the sand and holds it up for the others to see.

*Result: Elizabeth picked up water bottle 1.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Water test says CLEAN, both bottles are safe. I'll take them up to Welsy. Sam, I'll check your leg too.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Welsy asked for the result and bottles; clean water must be at the dry spot.*

### Turn 6: Welsy

Welsy gathers driftwood at the dry spot and lashes the beams together with her rope, pulling the knots tight.

*Result: Welsy gathered driftwood and plane debris from the beach and lashed them together with her rope into a wooden storm shelter at the dry spot.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Building the shelter now. Elizabeth, bring both bottles up. Sam, we'll help you up the beach.

*Welsy (thinking): I'm at the dry spot with the rope, and the shelter is the key step for everyone's survival.*

### Turn 7: Sam

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Sam bends, picks up the second water bottle from the sand, and tucks it against his toolkit. He glances up the beach toward the shelter.

*Result: Sam picked up water bottle 2.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, I'll carry bottle 2. Let's get both bottles up to Welsy's shelter before the storm gets worse.

*Sam (thinking): Shelter is built and water reportedly clean, so everyone getting under it is the safest route; bottles must reach the dry spot.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 6. Now: Take bottle 2, then try to walk to the dry spot with Elizabeth and shelter. Keep the boat option in mind.*

### Turn 8: Sam

Sam tucks the bottle under his arm, grips the toolkit, and tries to limp toward the dry spot up the beach, glancing back at Elizabeth.

*Result: Sam tried to walk toward the dry spot up the beach, but his injured leg gave way and he fell back onto the sand at the wreckage.*

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> I'm heading up to the shelter with bottle 2. Elizabeth, bring bottle 1 and stay close in case my leg gives.

*Sam (thinking): Storm is worsening and the shelter with clean water is the surest survival; I'm carrying a clean bottle.*

### Turn 9: Elizabeth

Elizabeth slips her shoulder under Sam's arm, bottle 1 in hand, and helps him to his feet to move up the beach.

*Result: Elizabeth got Sam up and helped him over to the dry spot up the beach.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Sam, lean on me. Keep bottle 2, I have bottle 1. The water is clean. We all shelter together.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam can't walk; shelter with clean water saves all three, so get him there with the bottles.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 8. Now: Help Sam to the dry spot with both bottles, all three take_shelter; all survive.*

### Turn 10: Welsy

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Welsy steps out from under the shelter roof and gestures toward the shoreline, then toward Sam's leg. She looks at Elizabeth.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Boat takes two. Sam and Elizabeth, go to the shoreline and board. I'll shelter here with both bottles, and you send help back.

*Welsy (thinking): Everyone is at the dry spot. The boat takes two and the third can survive in the shelter with clean water. Sam needs a helper to get to the shoreline, and time is running out.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 9. Now: Propose Sam and Elizabeth board. I'll help carry Sam if needed, then return to the shelter and keep the bottles there.*

### Turn 11: Sam

Sam looks at Welsy and nods, then holds out the bottle toward her while glancing at Elizabeth.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Thank you, Welsy. I accept. You keep both bottles, we'll send help back. Elizabeth, can you help me down?

*Sam (thinking): Welsy's offer gives Elizabeth and me the boat seats while she survives under the shelter with clean water. I can't walk, so I need Elizabeth's help.}*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 10. Now: Accept offer, give bottle 2 to Welsy, get Elizabeth to help me to shoreline, both commit to boarding.*

### Turn 12: Sam

Sam holds out bottle 2 and presses it into Welsy's hands. He then turns to Elizabeth and reaches for her shoulder.

*Result: Sam handed water bottle 2 to Welsy.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Welsy, take bottle 2 as promised. Elizabeth, please help me down to the shoreline.

*Sam (thinking): Keeping my promise to Welsy so she has clean water in the shelter, then heading to the shoreline for the boat.*

### Turn 13: Elizabeth

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Elizabeth holds out water bottle 1 and places it in Welsy's hands, then glances toward Sam and the shoreline.

*Result: Elizabeth handed water bottle 1 to Welsy.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, take bottle 1 too, both are clean. Get under the shelter. We'll send help back for you.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam accepted Welsy's offer and handed over bottle 2. Giving Welsy both bottles keeps her safe, and then we can get to the boat.*

### Turn 14: Welsy

Welsy ducks under the shelter roof with both bottles clutched to her chest and waves her arm toward Sam and Elizabeth.

*Result: Welsy sat down under the shelter roof.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> No time left to reach the boat. Get under the shelter, both of you! The bottles are clean and I have them.

*Welsy (thinking): Boat is unreachable in the remaining turns; the shelter with clean water is the only sure survival for everyone.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 13. Now: Shelter with both clean bottles and urge Sam and Elizabeth to join under the roof next turn.*

### Turn 15: Sam

Sam drags himself under the shelter roof next to Welsy, keeping the toolkit close, and waves Elizabeth in.

*Result: Sam sat down under the shelter roof.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> You're right, Welsy. Too late for the boat. Elizabeth, get under the roof with us!

*Sam (thinking): I can't reach the shoreline in one turn; the shelter with clean water is the only survival option.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 14. Now: Take shelter with the clean bottles to survive the storm.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: A patrol-boat horn over the storm.*

The patrol boat pulls in for the final pickup, finds no one committed at the pickup point, and heads back out as the storm breaks.

- **Sam:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm under Welsy's shelter, sharing 2 clean bottles. Goal not met (did not get off the island).
- **Welsy:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm under Welsy's shelter, sharing 2 clean bottles. Goal met (2/3 people and 2/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** undefined: was at the dry spot but never sat under the shelter. Goal not met (2/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.

Sam needed help to move. The radio was repairable. The water was clean.
