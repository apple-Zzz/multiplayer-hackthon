# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-11` (showcase, scenario seed 6319).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Sam

Sam crouches by the wreckage and opens his toolkit, examining the radio's casing and wiring.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Let me look at the radio first. Welsy, Elizabeth, the shelter and water are on you two.

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio WORKS. It only needs someone to send a distress call.*

*Sam (thinking): Knowing if the radio works decides everything; I am the only one who can do it.*

### Turn 2: Sam

Sam leans to the radio, presses the transmit key and speaks into the handset.

*Result: Sam sent a distress call. A voice crackled back: "Patrol boat confirms: two places at the shoreline pickup point, after the storm front, final pickup."*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> The radio works! Sending the distress call now. Boat takes two at the shoreline after the storm starts.

*Sam (thinking): Diagnosis showed the radio works and only needs a call; no boat comes without it.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 1. Now: Call rescue now, then tell others about the two-seat boat, the shelter, and water testing.*

### Turn 3: Welsy

Welsy scoops up one water bottle and nods toward the other bottle for Elizabeth, then glances up the beach.

*Result: Welsy picked up water bottle 1.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, grab the other bottle. I'll build a shelter at the dry spot, and we should get Sam moving.

*Welsy (thinking): Radio call succeeded; storm is coming so water and shelter are priority.*

### Turn 4: Welsy

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Welsy slips under Sam's arm, rope coiled on her shoulder, and helps him up toward the dry spot. She nods to Elizabeth to follow.

*Result: Welsy got Sam up, looping the rope under his arms, and helped him over to the dry spot up the beach.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Sam, lean on me. Elizabeth, bring bottle 2 and come along. We're going to the dry spot.

*Welsy (thinking): Time is limited and the shelter must be built at the dry spot, so I need to get Sam moving now.*

### Turn 5: Welsy

Welsy gathers driftwood and lashes it into a low roof frame with her rope.

*Result: Welsy gathered driftwood and plane debris from the beach and lashed them together with her rope into a wooden storm shelter at the dry spot.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Building the shelter now. Elizabeth, bring bottle 2 here, then we all get under it.

*Welsy (thinking): Sam is at the dry spot and wood is available, so the shelter is the key step to survive the storm.*

### Turn 6: Sam

Sam leans against the shelter post and gestures between the shoreline and the roof, looking at Welsy and then down the beach toward Elizabeth.

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Boat takes two at the shoreline, shelter saves the third if water's clean. Elizabeth, bring bottle 2 here. Let's decide together.

*Sam (thinking): Others may not fully know the boat limit; coordination is needed before anyone commits, and the water is unverified.*

### Turn 7: Elizabeth

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Elizabeth kneels by bottle 2, opens her first-aid kit and prepares a test strip.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Give me a moment. I'm testing this water before anyone relies on it.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CONTAMINATED. Neither bottle is safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Everything depends on whether the water is clean, and only I can test it.*

### Turn 8: Elizabeth

Elizabeth holds up the used test strip so the others can see it, then calls up the beach.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Both bottles are contaminated, I tested them. The shelter won't save anyone. The boat is our only way. Let's choose the two now.

*Elizabeth (thinking): The water is contaminated, so the shelter is useless and only the two boat places can save lives. The others need this to decide.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 7. Now: Share the result, agree who takes the two boat places (Sam's mobility matters), head to the shoreline, and make sure help is sent back for whoever stays.*

### Turn 9: Welsy

Welsy loops the rope under Sam's arms, keeps the bottle, and helps him toward the shoreline. She waves Elizabeth to follow.

*Result: Welsy got Sam up and helped him over to the shoreline pickup point.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, I can't test it myself, so I'll trust you. Sam, lean on me. We go to the shoreline. Elizabeth, meet us there.

*Welsy (thinking): Elizabeth reports contaminated water, so the shelter is unreliable and the boat is the only sure survival. Everyone needs to be at the shoreline.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 8. Now: Get Sam to the shoreline, gather everyone there, settle who boards fairly, and keep the promise to send help back.*

### Turn 10: Sam

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Sam braces against the wind at the waterline, plants himself at the pickup point and raises a hand to signal he is committing to board. He looks to Welsy.

*Result: Sam planted himself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Welsy, I'm committing to the boat. Take the second seat. Elizabeth, I promise we'll send help back for you right away.

*Sam (thinking): Water reported contaminated, so the boat is the only sure survival. Seats go to the first two committed, and I'm already at the shoreline.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 9. Now: Secure a boat seat now, urge Welsy to take the other, and send help back for whoever stays.*

### Turn 11: Sam

Sam grips the toolkit and looks at Welsy through the driving rain, nodding toward the waiting spot beside him.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Welsy, commit to the boat now before Elizabeth gets here. We'll send help back for her right away.

*Sam (thinking): Only two seats go to the first two to commit. Welsy hasn't committed yet and Elizabeth could take the seat first.*

### Turn 12: Elizabeth

Elizabeth grips the first-aid kit and hurries through the rain toward the shoreline.

*Result: Elizabeth walked from the wreckage to the shoreline pickup point.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> I'm coming to the shoreline. Welsy, Sam, please wait. Let's settle the second seat fairly, and send help back for whoever stays.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam is committed and wants Welsy to take the second seat before I arrive. The only way to take part in the decision is to be at the shoreline.*

### Turn 13: Welsy

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Welsy lowers Sam's arm from her shoulder, steps onto the pickup mark beside him, and faces the water. She glances back at Elizabeth.

*Result: Welsy planted herself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Time's nearly gone. I'm committing, Elizabeth. I'll send help back for you first thing. I'm sorry.

*Welsy (thinking): Only two turns remain. The water is reported contaminated, so the shelter can't save anyone and the boat is the only way to survive. Sam already holds one seat.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 12. Now: Board with Sam, then send help back for Elizabeth as promised.*

### Turn 14: Elizabeth

Elizabeth steadies herself against the wind and looks at Sam and Welsy. She holds out the first-aid kit toward Sam.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Sam, Welsy, go. Please send help back for me the moment you land. Tell them I'm at the shoreline.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Both seats are committed and the water is contaminated, so the best remaining outcome is two rescued and a firm promise to send help back for me.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 13. Now: Accept that the seats are taken, get a firm promise of rescue, and hand Sam the kit for his leg. Remain at the shoreline in case a seat opens.*

### Turn 15: Sam

Sam grips the toolkit and looks at Elizabeth, nodding solemnly. He steadies himself against Welsy as the waves reach the sand.

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, thank you. I swear we'll send help straight back for you at the shoreline. Hold on.

*Sam (thinking): Elizabeth agreed to stay; Sam and Welsy are committed, so accept and keep the promise to send help back.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: A patrol-boat horn over the storm.*

The patrol boat pulls in for the final pickup; Sam and Welsy board and it heads out as the storm breaks. As promised, they will send help back for Elizabeth.

- **Sam:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (got off the island alive).
- **Welsy:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (2/3 people and 1/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** undefined: was at the shoreline but never committed to boarding. Goal not met (2/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.
