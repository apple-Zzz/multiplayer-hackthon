# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-01`.*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Sam

Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'm a mechanic, so I'll check the radio. Elizabeth, can you test the water and look at my leg? Welsy, keep that rope ready.

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio is REPAIRABLE — a cracked solder joint and a loose antenna lead.*

*Sam (thinking): First plan. Only I can diagnose the radio, and it's the only way off the island, so I check it first.*

### Turn 2: Welsy

Welsy made an offer to everyone: "Elizabeth, test the water first. If it's clean I'll carry bottles to the dry spot. Sam, if your leg's bad I'll rope you up and move you wherever you need."

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Rope's ready. Elizabeth, test those bottles first. Sam, say the word and I'll get you moving. We have to beat this storm.

*Welsy (thinking): First plan. Sam asked me to keep the rope ready and wants Elizabeth to test the water and check the leg. I'm leaving the bottles alone so Elizabeth can test them first, and offering to help so we coordinate instead of rushing.*

### Turn 3: Elizabeth

Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Testing the water now. Sam, your leg is next. Welsy, hold off on carrying anything until I know what we've got.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CLEAN. Both bottles are safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Both Sam and Welsy asked me to test the water. Knowing whether it's clean tells us if the dry spot is a real fallback, and that shapes everyone's plan.*

### Turn 4: Welsy

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Welsy looked over the radio.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Holding off on the bottles, Elizabeth. What did the test show? Sam, what's wrong with the radio? I'm taking a look too.

*Only Welsy learns this: The radio is dead: no power, no light.*

*Welsy (thinking): Elizabeth asked me to hold off on carrying, and neither Sam's radio check nor Elizabeth's test result has been shared. Inspecting the radio myself gets me confirmed information without taking anything.*

### Turn 5: Elizabeth

Elizabeth knelt by Sam and examined Sam's injured leg.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> Water is CLEAN, both bottles. Two can take the boat and one can wait it out dry. All three of us can make it. Sam, let me see that leg.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Assessment: Sam's leg is sprained but bears weight. Sam CAN WALK independently.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): The water is confirmed clean and Welsy asked for the result, so I'm sharing it honestly. Next I need Sam's leg status to decide who goes where.*

### Turn 6: Sam

Sam worked on the radio with the toolkit. Its indicator light flickered on and static hissed from the speaker.

**SAM** *(to Welsy)*
> Radio's fixable: cracked solder joint and a loose antenna lead. Repairing now, then I'll call. Welsy, I'll need your rope to get to shore.

*Only Sam learns this: Repair succeeded: the radio powers on and can transmit.*

*Sam (thinking): My diagnosis says the radio is repairable, and I only have a few turns left, so the repair can't wait. Once I call, the shoreline pickup is set up and the dry-spot backup covers the third person.*

### Turn 7: Elizabeth

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Elizabeth sent a distress call. A voice crackled back: "Patrol boat confirms — two places, shoreline pickup point, before the storm."

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Calling it in now. Sam, your leg is just a sprain and you can walk on your own. Sam and Welsy take the boat; I'll wait it out dry with a bottle.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam repaired the radio on turn 6, so I can make the call myself right now and save Sam an action. Sam can walk, so nobody needs to spend turns helping Sam move.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 6. Now: Send the distress call now so the boat is confirmed. Sam and Welsy walk to the shoreline and commit to boarding. I take a clean bottle and go to the dry spot with my last two actions.*

### Turn 8: Sam

Sam walked from the wreckage to the shoreline pickup point, limping but unaided.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Thanks, Elizabeth. Heading to the shoreline now. Welsy, follow me down and keep the rope handy in case this leg gives out.

*Sam (thinking): Elizabeth's distress call on turn 7 worked, so I don't need to call any more. Elizabeth says my leg can carry me, but that's only her word, and trying to walk is the quickest way to find out while I still have turns left for Welsy to help.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 7. Now: Walk to the shoreline now to test the leg. If it fails, accept Welsy's rope help on turn 10. Then commit to boarding on turn 15, with Welsy taking the second place and Elizabeth waiting it out at the dry spot with a clean bottle.*

### Turn 9: Welsy

Welsy walked from the wreckage to the shoreline pickup point.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Good work, both of you. Elizabeth, take a clean bottle up to the dry spot now, don't wait on the rain. I'm going down to Sam with the rope.

*Welsy (thinking): On turn 7 the call succeeded with two boat places and Elizabeth proposed Sam and I board while Elizabeth waits dry. Sam can walk unaided (turn 8), so I don't need to carry Sam. Moving now leaves me two actions to commit to boarding, and Elizabeth can carry her own bottle.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 7. Now: Join Sam at the shoreline and commit to boarding so Sam and I take the two boat places while Elizabeth waits out the storm at the dry spot with a clean bottle. Keep the rope ready in case Sam's leg gives out.*

### Turn 10: Sam

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Sam planted themselves at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'm committed to the boat. Welsy, commit too, that's both places. Elizabeth, take a clean bottle up to the dry spot before the storm hits.

*Sam (thinking): The call went through and I walked to the shoreline on my own. The first two to commit get the boat places, so I'm committing now while the plan Elizabeth proposed still has Welsy and me on board.*

### Turn 11: Welsy

Welsy planted themselves at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> I'm committed, Sam, that's both places. Elizabeth, it's two moves for you: grab a bottle, then get up to the dry spot. Go now!

*Welsy (thinking): Sam committed on turn 10 and asked me to take the second place, which follows the plan Elizabeth proposed. Committing now secures both boat places, and Elizabeth still has turns 12 and 14 to take a bottle and reach the dry spot.*

### Turn 12: Elizabeth

Elizabeth picked up water bottle 1.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Got a clean bottle. Heading up to the dry spot next. Hold tight down there, both of you.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Plan is on track: Sam and Welsy committed on turns 10 and 11, so I just need the bottle and then the dry spot.*

### Turn 13: Welsy

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Welsy held position at the shoreline pickup point.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Nice work, Elizabeth! One more move and you're up at the dry spot. Sam, we hold here. Boat's coming.

*Welsy (thinking): Elizabeth picked up a clean bottle on turn 12 and said she's heading to the dry spot, with turn 14 left to get there. Elizabeth isn't stalling, so I'm keeping my committed place and holding position.*

### Turn 14: Elizabeth

Elizabeth walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Made it up with clean water. Get on that boat, both of you. See you after the storm!

*Elizabeth (thinking): Final step of the plan: Sam and Welsy are committed at the shoreline, and I have a clean bottle, so moving to the dry spot gets me through the storm too.*

### Turn 15: Sam

Sam held position at the shoreline pickup point.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Holding here with Welsy. Stay dry up there, Elizabeth, and thank you. We'll send help back for you as soon as the storm passes.

*Sam (thinking): Everyone has a way through: Welsy and I have the two committed boat places, and Elizabeth reached the dry spot on turn 14 holding a bottle she says is clean. Moving or giving up my place would only put me at risk.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: A patrol-boat horn over the storm.*

An offscreen patrol-boat horn sounds; Sam and Welsy wade out of frame toward it as the storm breaks over the beach.

- **Sam:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat. Goal met (got off the island alive).
- **Welsy:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat. Goal met (3/3 people reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm at the dry spot with a clean bottle (v1 rules: no shelter needed). Goal met (3/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.
