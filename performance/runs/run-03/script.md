# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-03` (showcase, scenario seed 109).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels by the bottles on the sand and opens the first-aid kit. She tests a sample from the first bottle, holding the strip up to the light.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Before anyone drinks anything, I'm testing these bottles with my kit. Give me a moment.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CLEAN. Both bottles are safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Water safety decides survival at the shelter and nobody else has checked it; it is the most time-critical private fact.*

### Turn 2: Sam

Sam kneels by the wreckage, pulls the radio's casing open with his toolkit, and studies the wiring and circuit board closely.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, test away. I'll see if the radio can be fixed while you work.

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio is REPAIRABLE (a cracked solder joint and a loose antenna lead).*

*Sam (thinking): A working radio is our only route off the island, and checking it costs nothing while Elizabeth tests the water.*

### Turn 3: Sam

Sam kneels by the open radio casing, sets the toolkit beside him, and reseats the antenna lead. He solders the cracked joint with careful, steady hands.

*Result: Sam worked on the radio with the toolkit. Its indicator light flickered on and static hissed from the speaker.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> It's repairable: a cracked solder joint and a loose antenna lead. I'm fixing it now.

*Only Sam learns this: Repair succeeded: the radio powers on and can transmit.*

*Sam (thinking): Diagnosis says it is repairable, and a working radio is the fastest route off the island.*

### Turn 4: Welsy

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Welsy steps to the radio, keeping the rope over one shoulder, and leans in to the speaker. She presses the call switch and holds it, watching the indicator.

*Result: Welsy sent a distress call. A voice crackled back: "Patrol boat confirms: two places at the shoreline pickup point, after the storm front, final pickup."*

**WELSY** *(to Sam)*
> Sam, the radio's lit. Let's get a distress call out before the wind builds.

*Welsy (thinking): Sam's repair means the radio can now call; the storm is coming and a patrol boat is our way out.*

### Turn 5: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels beside Sam, sets the first-aid kit open on the sand, and gently checks his injured leg, watching his face as she presses along the shin.

*Result: Elizabeth knelt by Sam and examined his injured leg.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> Both bottles are clean, so nobody drinks from anything else. Sam, let me look at your leg before we decide who walks where.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Assessment: Sam's leg cannot bear weight. He NEEDS HELP to move to another spot.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): The radio works and the boat takes two committed people at the shoreline; Sam's mobility decides who can reach the pickup point in time.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 4. Now: Share that water is clean, then learn whether Sam can walk to the shoreline before planning boarding and shelter.*

### Turn 6: Elizabeth

Elizabeth slides under Sam's arm on his uninjured side and takes his weight. She steadies him and begins guiding him slowly toward the shoreline.

*Result: Elizabeth got Sam up and helped him over to the shoreline pickup point.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> Sam, I've got your good side. We're getting you to the shoreline now, before the wind builds.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam cannot bear weight and the boat takes committed people at the shoreline, so he has to be moved now.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 5. Now: Get Sam to the shoreline for boarding, then decide who else boards and whether Welsy builds shelter with a clean bottle for whoever stays.*

### Turn 7: Sam

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Sam plants his good leg at the shoreline pickup point, sets the toolkit down beside him, and turns to face the water where the boat will come.

*Result: Sam planted himself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'm committed to the boat. I'm standing here at the pickup point. Welsy, Elizabeth, come up when you can.

*Sam (thinking): The call succeeded and only the first two committed people board, so I should lock in a place now while I am already at the shoreline.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 4. Now: Commit to boarding at the shoreline now, since the distress call succeeded. Then get Welsy and Elizabeth to the pickup point and make sure the boat's two places are used well.*

### Turn 8: Elizabeth

Elizabeth sets the first-aid kit down at the pickup point and stands square beside Sam, facing the wreckage. She raises her hand toward Welsy.

*Result: Elizabeth planted herself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> I'm committing to the boat too, Sam. That's the two places. Welsy, you're the one who can build the shelter.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam is committed and the boat takes two. I am the second committed person, which secures both places and leaves shelter for Welsy.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 7. Now: Board with Sam on the two committed places. Welsy builds shelter at the dry spot and keeps one clean bottle.*

### Turn 9: Welsy

Welsy crouches by the sand, lifts the first water bottle, checks the seal, and tucks it under her arm with the rope.

*Result: Welsy picked up water bottle 1.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> The call's out and the boat's coming. Elizabeth says both bottles are clean, so I'm taking one for the shelter.

*Welsy (thinking): Boat places are already committed by Sam and Elizabeth; the shelter needs a clean bottle held by someone at the finished shelter.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 4. Now: Take a clean bottle, carry it to the dry spot, and build the storm shelter with rope and beach wood before the storm hits.*

### Turn 10: Elizabeth

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Elizabeth lifts her arm toward the dry spot up the beach and calls across the wind. She keeps her place at the shoreline pickup point beside Sam.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Welsy, the boat has two places and Sam and I have them. Please build the shelter at the dry spot and keep your clean bottle.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Only two boat places exist and Sam and I hold them. Welsy's survival depends on building the shelter and keeping her clean bottle, so I need her to act on that now.*

### Turn 11: Elizabeth

Elizabeth stays planted at the shoreline with the first-aid kit and looks up the beach toward Welsy. She points toward the dry spot and raises her voice over the wind.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, the storm's closing in. Please build the shelter at the dry spot now and hold on to your clean bottle.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Welsy hasn't answered my offer yet. The shelter is the only way she survives, and the storm is about to hit, so I need her to commit to building it now.*

### Turn 12: Welsy

Welsy shoulders the coiled rope, keeps her clean bottle tucked against her side, and walks up the beach toward the dry spot, glancing back at the wind-whipped shoreline.

*Result: Welsy walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, I accept. I'm taking my clean bottle and the rope up to the dry spot to build the shelter now.

*Welsy (thinking): Shelter is the only thing that protects me after turn 15, and building requires me at the dry spot. Moving first is the critical step; accepting the offer aloud can follow.*

### Turn 13: Welsy

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Welsy gathers driftwood from the beach at the dry spot and starts lashing the pieces together with her rope, keeping the clean bottle tucked against her side. She glances down the beach toward the shoreline.

*Result: Welsy gathered driftwood and plane debris from the beach and lashed them together with her rope into a wooden storm shelter at the dry spot.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, I'm building it now. Keep your eyes on the water, I've got the shelter.

*Welsy (thinking): Storm is on top of us; the shelter is the only way I survive, and I already accepted Elizabeth's offer.*

### Turn 14: Welsy

Welsy stands inside the shelter's frame with the clean bottle held against her chest. She looks down the beach toward the shoreline and raises her free hand once.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> The shelter's up and my clean bottle's with me. I'm staying here. Get on that boat, you two.

*Welsy (thinking): Shelter is built and my clean bottle is in hand; moving now only risks losing the bottle or the shelter's protection.*

### Turn 15: Sam

Sam keeps his place at the shoreline pickup point, toolkit at his side, and calls up the beach to Welsy. He looks steadily toward the dry spot.

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Welsy, stay in the shelter with your clean bottle. Elizabeth and I are set here at the pickup point.

*Sam (thinking): Both boat places are already committed to Sam and Elizabeth, and Welsy's clean bottle and shelter keep her alive through the storm. Moving now would risk everyone.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: A patrol-boat horn over the storm.*

The patrol boat pulls in for the final pickup; Sam and Elizabeth board and it heads out as the storm breaks.

- **Sam:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (got off the island alive).
- **Welsy:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm in Welsy's shelter with a clean bottle. Goal met (3/3 people and 1/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (3/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.
