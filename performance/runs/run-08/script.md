# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-08` (showcase, scenario seed 98213).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Sam

Sam crouches stiffly by the wreckage, opens his toolkit and starts examining the radio's casing and wiring.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Let me look at the radio first. If it's fixable, that's our way off. Welsy, Elizabeth, hang tight.

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio is BROKEN BEYOND REPAIR (the transmitter board is burnt through). No distress call is possible, so no boat can come.*

*Sam (thinking): Knowing whether the radio can work determines the whole strategy; I'm the only one who can fix it.*

### Turn 2: Sam

Sam closes the radio casing and sets down his screwdriver, shaking his head. He looks to Welsy and Elizabeth and points up the beach toward the dry spot.

**SAM** *(to Welsy)*
> The radio's burnt through, it can't be fixed. No boat. Shelter is our only chance, Welsy. Bring the bottles up the beach.

*Sam (thinking): Diagnosis showed the radio is unrepairable, so survival depends on shelter and clean water; honest sharing helps coordination.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 1. Now: Tell them radio is dead, push shelter at dry spot with both bottles carried there; get Elizabeth to assess my leg and try moving to dry spot.*

### Turn 3: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels beside the opened radio casing and looks over the burnt circuitry carefully.

*Result: Elizabeth looked over the radio.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> Sam, I'd like to see the radio myself before we give up on it. One quick look.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: The radio is dead: no power, no light.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam's claim that the radio is dead is unconfirmed, and a working radio means rescue for two people. It is worth a single turn to check.*

### Turn 4: Welsy

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Welsy crouches, picks up water bottle 1 and tucks it under her arm, glancing at the darkening sky and then at the others.

*Result: Welsy picked up water bottle 1.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, check the radio fast. I'm taking a bottle up the beach to build the shelter. Sam, Elizabeth, bring the other.

*Welsy (thinking): Time is limited and the shelter is my unique contribution; I need water at the dry spot, and Elizabeth is already checking the radio.*

### Turn 5: Sam

Sam picks up the second bottle from the sand and tucks it against his chest. He glances at Elizabeth and nods toward the dry spot.

*Result: Sam picked up water bottle 2.*

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, the radio's dead, I checked twice. Let's go. I'm taking bottle 2 up the beach to the shelter.

*Sam (thinking): Welsy is already heading to build shelter; the second bottle must reach the dry spot and shelter is the only way to survive.*

### Turn 6: Elizabeth

Elizabeth opens her first-aid kit and takes out a test strip. She steps toward Sam's bottle to test it.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> You're right, the radio's dead. Before we haul bottles up, let me test the water. It matters for the shelter.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CLEAN. Both bottles are safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): The radio is confirmed dead, so shelter with clean water is the only way to survive. Water safety decides it.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 5. Now: Test water, then assess Sam's leg, then go to the dry spot and share results.*

### Turn 7: Elizabeth

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Elizabeth holds up the test strip, then slips an arm under Sam's shoulder and starts helping him toward the dry spot.

*Result: Elizabeth got Sam up and helped him over to the dry spot up the beach.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Water's clean, both bottles. Welsy, build the shelter. Sam, lean on me, I'll help you up the beach.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Radio is dead and water is clean, so the shelter is the only way to survive. Sharing the result gets everyone moving.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 6. Now: Help Sam to the dry spot, then take shelter once Welsy builds it. Keep the bottles with us.*

### Turn 8: Sam

Sam lowers himself beside Elizabeth, cradling the bottle, and waves Welsy toward the dry spot.

**SAM** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, water's clean and bottle 2 is here. Bring your rope up and build the shelter, the storm's coming!

*Sam (thinking): Water is confirmed clean and only Welsy can build the shelter; the radio is dead, so shelter is the only survival route.*

### Turn 9: Elizabeth

Elizabeth cups her hands to her mouth and calls down the beach, one arm waving Welsy toward the dry spot while Sam rests beside her.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, both bottles are clean, I tested them. Bring the rope and bottle 1 up here and build the shelter, please!

*Elizabeth (thinking): The radio is confirmed dead, so shelter is the only way to survive. Welsy is still at the wreckage and must come build it.*

### Turn 10: Welsy

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Welsy crouches beside the open radio casing, rain streaming off her jacket, and examines the circuitry quickly. She keeps the bottle and rope tight against her.

*Result: Welsy looked over the radio.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> I'm right here at the radio. One quick look, then I'm bringing the rope up.

*Only Welsy learns this: The radio is dead: no power, no light.*

*Welsy (thinking): Only I am at the wreckage and the radio claim is unverified; I have a spare turn before the shelter must be built.*

### Turn 11: Welsy

Welsy turns from the wreckage, tucks the rope over her shoulder, grips the bottle, and pushes up the beach through the rain.

*Result: Welsy walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Radio's dead, you were right. I'm coming up with the rope and bottle 1. Shelter next.

*Welsy (thinking): Radio confirmed dead; shelter is the only way to survive, and I must carry rope and bottle 1 there.*

### Turn 12: Welsy

Welsy kneels in the driving rain, gathers driftwood and lashes it together with her rope into a low roofed shelter.

*Result: Welsy gathered driftwood and plane debris from the beach and lashed them together with her rope into a wooden storm shelter at the dry spot.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Sam, Elizabeth, stay close. I'm lashing the shelter now, then everyone get under it.

*Welsy (thinking): All three are at the dry spot with both clean bottles. Building the shelter is the critical step.*

### Turn 13: Elizabeth

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Elizabeth ducks under the lashed roof and sits down, motioning Sam and Welsy to join her.

*Result: Elizabeth sat down under the shelter roof.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Shelter's up and both bottles are clean. Sam, Welsy, get under the roof now, stay together.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Shelter is complete, both bottles are clean and at the dry spot, so sheltering saves everyone.*

### Turn 14: Sam

Sam limps under the shelter roof and sits down, keeping bottle 2 close beside him. He waves Welsy in.

*Result: Sam sat down under the shelter roof.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy)*
> Shelter's up, water's clean. I'm getting under the roof now. Welsy, join us, stay together.

*Sam (thinking): Shelter is complete, water is clean, and I'm at the dry spot; taking shelter is the only way to survive.*

### Turn 15: Welsy

Welsy ducks under the lashed roof and sits down beside Sam and Elizabeth, bottle 1 held close.

*Result: Welsy sat down under the shelter roof.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Getting under the roof now. We all stay together with both clean bottles.

*Welsy (thinking): Final turn: sitting under the shelter is the only way I survive.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: Thunder cracks overhead.*

No boat is coming. The storm breaks over the beach.

- **Sam:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm under Welsy's shelter, sharing 2 clean bottles. Goal not met (did not get off the island).
- **Welsy:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm under Welsy's shelter, sharing 2 clean bottles. Goal met (3/3 people and 2/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** sheltered and survived: waited out the storm under Welsy's shelter, sharing 2 clean bottles. Goal met (3/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.
