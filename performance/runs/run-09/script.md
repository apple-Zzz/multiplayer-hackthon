# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-09` (showcase, scenario seed 25517).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Sam

Sam lowers himself beside the wreckage, opens his toolkit, and starts examining the radio's casing and wiring.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Let me look at the radio first. If it can be fixed, that's our ticket out. Welsy, Elizabeth, hang tight.

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio WORKS. It only needs someone to send a distress call.*

*Sam (thinking): Radio is the key to rescue and only I can work on it; knowing if it's fixable decides everything.*

### Turn 2: Sam

Sam leans over the open radio, adjusts a dial and presses the transmit key, speaking into the handset.

*Result: Sam sent a distress call. A voice crackled back: "Patrol boat confirms: two places at the shoreline pickup point, after the storm front, final pickup."*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Good news, the radio works! I'm sending the distress call now. Welsy, please build a shelter. Elizabeth, check the water.

*Sam (thinking): Radio works, so the call is what brings the boat; it must be sent early.*

### Turn 3: Welsy

Welsy coils the rope over her shoulder and sets off up the beach toward the dry spot, glancing back at the others.

*Result: Welsy walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> I'm heading to the dry spot to build the shelter. Elizabeth, check the water and bring the bottles up. Sam, we'll get you moving.

*Welsy (thinking): Shelter must be built at the dry spot with my rope; Sam asked for it and the storm is coming.*

### Turn 4: Elizabeth

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Elizabeth kneels by the bottles, opens her first-aid kit and prepares a test on one bottle.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Checking the water now, before anyone drinks or carries it.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CLEAN. Both bottles are safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Water safety decides whether the shelter works; the call already succeeded, so shelter and water are the priority.*

### Turn 5: Welsy

Welsy gathers driftwood at the dry spot and lashes it into a low roof with her rope.

*Result: Welsy gathered driftwood and plane debris from the beach and lashed them together with her rope into a wooden storm shelter at the dry spot.*

**WELSY** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Building the shelter now. Elizabeth, tell us what the water test says, then bring the bottles up.

*Welsy (thinking): The shelter is only useful if it's built early, and only I can build it.*

### Turn 6: Sam

Sam turns to Elizabeth, wiping his hands on a rag, and gestures at the two bottles on the sand.

**SAM** *(to Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, what did the water test say? Tell us, then take the bottles up to the shelter. Then check my leg.

*Sam (thinking): Elizabeth tested the water but never reported the result. Everyone needs it before the bottles are moved.*

### Turn 7: Elizabeth

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Elizabeth crouches, picks up one water bottle and tucks it under her arm, glancing at Sam.

*Result: Elizabeth picked up water bottle 1.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Sam, Welsy: the water test came back CLEAN. Both bottles are safe. I'm taking them up, then I'll check your leg.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Clean water makes the shelter work; bottles must reach the dry spot, and Sam asked me to share the result.*

### Turn 8: Welsy

Welsy coils the spare rope over her shoulder and heads down the beach toward the wreckage, leaning into the gusts.

*Result: Welsy walked from the dry spot up the beach to the wreckage.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Shelter's done. I'm coming down to help Sam up. Elizabeth, bring bottle 1 up; I'll grab the other.

*Welsy (thinking): Water is reported clean and the shelter is built. Sam and bottle 2 are still at the wreckage, so I'll go and help move them.*

### Turn 9: Welsy

Welsy crouches and picks up the second water bottle from the sand, then offers her shoulder to Sam.

*Result: Welsy picked up water bottle 2.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> I've got bottle 2. Elizabeth, take bottle 1 up now. Sam, lean on me, we're going to the shelter.

*Welsy (thinking): Both clean bottles must reach the shelter so everyone survives; storm is intensifying.*

### Turn 10: Elizabeth

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Elizabeth kneels beside Sam, sets the first-aid kit down and carefully examines his injured leg.

*Result: Elizabeth knelt by Sam and examined his injured leg.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam)*
> Sam, hold still. Let me check your leg now, before we move anyone.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Assessment: Sam's leg is only BRUISED. He CAN WALK on his own.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam's mobility decides who can reach the shoreline. I'm beside him, and only 5 turns remain.*

### Turn 11: Elizabeth

Elizabeth rises from beside Sam, the water bottle in one hand, and gestures toward the shoreline and then up the beach to the shelter.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Sam's leg is only bruised, he can walk! Sam and Welsy, board the boat. I'll shelter with the water. Send help back for me.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam can walk, so two can board the boat and one shelters with clean water. All three survive if this is carried out, and I can take the shelter spot.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 10. Now: Share leg result. Sam and Welsy go to shoreline and commit. I take bottle 1 to the shelter and stay under the roof with clean water. Then confirm the pickup promise.*

### Turn 12: Sam

Sam grips the toolkit, pushes himself up and tests his weight on the injured leg. He limps toward the shoreline.

*Result: Sam walked from the wreckage to the shoreline pickup point, limping but unaided.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Thank you, Elizabeth. Deal. Welsy, let's go to the shoreline. We'll send help back for you, I promise.

*Sam (thinking): Elizabeth says the leg is only bruised. The boat takes two, and the shelter has clean water for her. Walking also tests her claim.*

### Turn 13: Welsy

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Welsy holds bottle 2 out and presses it into Elizabeth's hands, then nods toward the shelter up the beach.

*Result: Welsy handed water bottle 2 to Elizabeth.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, take bottle 2 too. Get both up to the shelter. Sam and I will go to the shoreline. We'll send help back for you.

*Welsy (thinking): Elizabeth's plan is workable: Sam can walk, boat takes two, she shelters with the water; I should give her the second bottle then go.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 11. Now: Hand bottle 2 to Elizabeth, then move to shoreline next turn and commit to boarding; Elizabeth shelters with the water.*

### Turn 14: Sam

Sam plants his feet at the pickup point, faces the water and signals toward Welsy to join him.

*Result: Sam planted himself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'm committed to the boat. Welsy, come to the shoreline and commit too. Elizabeth, we'll send help for you.

*Sam (thinking): Only one turn remains after this one, so I must secure a boat place now. Elizabeth agreed to shelter with the water.*

### Turn 15: Elizabeth

Elizabeth grips both bottles and her kit and hurries up the beach toward the dry spot, pointing Welsy toward the shoreline.

*Result: Elizabeth walked from the wreckage to the dry spot up the beach.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, run to the shoreline and commit now! I'm taking both clean bottles to the shelter.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Last turn: getting the clean water to the shelter is the only way I survive, and Welsy needs to commit to the boat.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: A patrol-boat horn over the storm.*

The patrol boat pulls in for the final pickup; Sam boards and it heads out as the storm breaks. As promised, they will send help back for Welsy and Elizabeth.

- **Sam:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (got off the island alive).
- **Welsy:** undefined: was still at the wreckage, exposed, when the storm hit. Goal not met (1/3 people and 0/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** undefined: was at the dry spot but never sat under the shelter. Goal not met (1/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.
