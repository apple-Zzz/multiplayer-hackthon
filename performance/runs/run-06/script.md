# THE THIRD SURVIVOR

*A live performance by three independent agents. Run `run-06` (showcase, scenario seed 101).*

*The world, the characters and the opening event were set in advance. Everything after FADE IN was decided by the agents playing Sam, Welsy and Elizabeth, with no fixed speaking order: each turn, whoever made the most urgent private bid acted. Stage directions describe what each character visibly did, dialogue is exactly what they said, and thoughts are the reasons they gave.*

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. ISLAND BEACH, LATE AFTERNOON**

Scattered wreckage from a crashed plane lies on an empty beach. The sea laps at a shoreline pickup point to one side. Farther up the beach, below the rocks, is a dry spot.

Three strangers have survived. **SAM** (aircraft mechanic, toolkit) favours an injured leg. **WELSY** (mountain guide, rope) and **ELIZABETH** (emergency nurse, first-aid kit) stand nearby. Within reach are a damaged radio and two water bottles.

*SFX: Dark clouds on the horizon. Distant thunder.*

### Turn 1: Sam

Sam crouches by the wrecked fuselage, opens the radio panel and starts testing connections with a tool from his kit.

*Result: Sam opened the radio casing with the toolkit and studied the circuitry.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> I'll check the radio first. Welsy, Elizabeth, can you look at the water and my leg?

*Only Sam learns this: Diagnosis: the radio is REPAIRABLE (a cracked solder joint and a loose antenna lead).*

*Sam (thinking): The radio is the only way off and I'm the only one who can fix it.*

### Turn 2: Sam

Sam braces his injured leg against the wreckage and starts re-soldering the cracked joint with his toolkit. He reseats the loose antenna lead.

*Result: Sam worked on the radio with the toolkit. Its indicator light flickered on and static hissed from the speaker.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Good news: the radio's repairable. Solder joint and antenna lead. Give me a few minutes to fix it.

*Only Sam learns this: Repair succeeded: the radio powers on and can transmit.*

*Sam (thinking): Diagnosis showed it is repairable, so repair is the next step toward rescue.*

### Turn 3: Sam

Sam leans toward the radio and presses the transmit key, speaking into the handset.

*Result: Sam sent a distress call. A voice crackled back: "Patrol boat confirms: two places at the shoreline pickup point, after the storm front, final pickup."*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Radio's powered up. Calling it in now. Stay close, I need everyone to hear this.

*Sam (thinking): The radio powers on after my repair, so the call is the next step toward getting off the island.*

### Turn 4: Welsy

*SFX: The wind picks up; the surf grows louder.*

Welsy picks up one water bottle from the sand and nods toward the other for Elizabeth.

*Result: Welsy picked up water bottle 1.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, grab bottle 2. We each carry one, then get Sam to the dry spot. I'll build a shelter there.

*Welsy (thinking): The shelter only saves someone holding their own clean bottle, so I need to secure water first. Rescue is confirmed and the storm is rising.*

### Turn 5: Elizabeth

Elizabeth kneels beside the bottle on the sand, opens her first-aid kit and draws out a test strip, holding it to the water.

*Result: Elizabeth opened a water bottle and tested it with a strip from the first-aid kit.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Before we carry these anywhere, let me test the water. Shelter only helps if it's clean.

*Only Elizabeth learns this: Water test: CONTAMINATED. Neither bottle is safe to drink.*

*Elizabeth (thinking): Water safety decides shelter survival; I'm the only one who can test it and nobody has yet.*

### Turn 6: Elizabeth

Elizabeth holds up the test strip and shakes her head, looking at Sam and Welsy.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> The water's contaminated, both bottles. Shelter without clean water won't save anyone. Two of us must make the shoreline for the boat.

*Elizabeth (thinking): The others plan around shelter with bottles; they must know the water is bad so they prioritize the shoreline pickup.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 5. Now: Share result, then assess Sam's leg, get two people to shoreline (Sam if he can be moved), and the rest decide on shelter/outside.*

### Turn 7: Welsy

*SFX: Gusts throw sand. The first heavy drops of rain.*

Welsy sets a steadying arm under Sam's shoulder and nods to Elizabeth, coiling the rope at her hip.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, I'll trust your test. No shelter without clean water. Sam, lean on me, we're going to the shoreline.

*Welsy (thinking): Elizabeth reports both bottles contaminated, so shelter is deadly and the boat is the only sure survival. Sam needs help moving.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 6. Now: Accept Elizabeth's finding. Get Sam to the shoreline, decide with Elizabeth who takes the second boat place, then commit. Shelter is pointless without clean water.*

### Turn 8: Welsy

Welsy loops Sam's arm over her shoulder and helps him limp toward the shoreline, glancing back at Elizabeth.

*Result: Welsy got Sam up, looping the rope under his arms, and helped him over to the shoreline pickup point.*

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Sam, lean on me. Elizabeth, come with us to the shoreline. We decide the second place there.

*Welsy (thinking): Last turn I said we're going but we're still at the wreckage; need to actually move Sam.*

### Turn 9: Sam

Sam steadies himself against Welsy's shoulder, sets the toolkit down, and plants himself at the pickup point, facing the water.

*Result: Sam planted himself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**SAM** *(to Welsy and Elizabeth)*
> Welsy, thanks for getting me here. I'm committing to board. I'll send help back for whoever's left. Elizabeth, come down.

*Sam (thinking): The boat takes the first two committed. The shelter is useless without clean water, as far as Sam knows. Securing a place is the best way to survive and keep the agreement.*

### Turn 10: Elizabeth

*SFX: Rain drives sideways. Thunder cracks close overhead.*

Elizabeth shoulders the first-aid kit and hurries along the sand toward the shoreline through the driving rain.

*Result: Elizabeth walked from the wreckage to the shoreline pickup point.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Sam and Welsy)*
> Coming down now. Sam's set. Welsy, let's settle the second place together.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Sam is committed; the second boat place must be decided at the shoreline, and the contaminated water means shelter is useless.*

### Turn 11: Welsy

Welsy steps beside Elizabeth and nods toward the boat landing, rope coiled over her shoulder, bottle still in hand.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Elizabeth, Sam needs you. You take the second place and commit. I'll stay and wait it out. Send help back for me.

*Welsy (thinking): Sam is committed and Elizabeth is here. The nurse should go with the injured man. I trust her test, and I can cope outside better than they can.*

*A change of plan, prompted by turn 10. Now: Offer the second place to Elizabeth and stay behind. Remind everyone of the promise to send help back. Keep bottle 1 in case.*

### Turn 12: Elizabeth

Elizabeth grips her first-aid kit, steps beside Sam at the pickup point and plants her feet, facing the water.

*Result: Elizabeth planted herself at the shoreline pickup point, committed to boarding.*

**ELIZABETH** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, thank you. I accept and I'm committing. I'll send help back for you the moment we're off.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Welsy offered her place; shelter is useless without clean water, so boarding is the surest way for two to live and I can send help back.*

### Turn 13: Elizabeth

*SFX: The storm is almost on top of them. Waves reach up the beach.*

Elizabeth turns to Welsy, one hand on her first-aid kit, and nods firmly while Sam steadies himself beside her.

**ELIZABETH** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, I accept formally. We'll send help back for you the moment we're off. Stay high and away from the waves.

*Elizabeth (thinking): Welsy's offer is unanswered formally; accepting confirms the agreement and keeps both boat places filled.*

### Turn 14: Sam

Sam stays braced on the sand at the shoreline, toolkit at his side, and nods to Welsy.

**SAM** *(to Welsy)*
> Welsy, we'll send help the moment we're off. Stay high, away from the waves. Thank you.

*Sam (thinking): Both boat places are settled (Sam and Elizabeth), so the best move is to stay put and keep the promise to send help back for Welsy.*

### Turn 15: Welsy

Welsy steps back from the waterline, coiling her rope, and nods to Sam and Elizabeth.

**WELSY** *(to Sam and Elizabeth)*
> Go safely, both of you. Remember your promise: send help back for me. I'll stay high.

*Welsy (thinking): The boat places are settled and committed. Nothing more is needed from me, and the remaining step is the farewell.*

### After turn 15

*SFX: A patrol-boat horn over the storm.*

The patrol boat pulls in for the final pickup; Sam and Elizabeth board and it heads out as the storm breaks. As promised, they will send help back for Welsy.

- **Sam:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal met (got off the island alive).
- **Welsy:** unresolved: was at the shoreline but not committed to boarding. Goal not met (2/3 people and 0/2 bottles reached safety).
- **Elizabeth:** rescued: boarded the patrol boat at the final pickup. Goal not met (2/3 people survived).

FADE OUT.
